Forager Lab

Each dot has a small neural network that decides which way to move, at any angle, and when to eat or drink. Nobody tells them how. Dots that find food and water split into mutated copies, and the rest die. A dot needs food for energy and water for hydration, and dies when either runs out.

Everything here comes in an energy and a hydration version with the same units, so the two can be compared directly. A dot dies at 0 of either. Set "Lost per tick" and "Extra lost per move" to 0 for energy or for hydration to switch that need off entirely, for a one-resource test. To split, a dot needs the "needed" amount of both, loses the "parent loses" amounts, and the child starts with the "child receives" amounts. The parent survives with what is left, and always keeps at least 1 of each. The amount the child receives is separate, so the difference is created or lost.

Tiles a dot travels in one tick at full speed, in any direction it chooses. A dot can also move slower or stand still. The "extra lost per move" amounts apply at full speed and shrink in proportion.
How much of its velocity a dot keeps each tick. The brain's move output becomes the velocity it asks for, and the dot's real velocity eases toward it. 0: moves exactly as the brain says, every tick. High: the dot keeps going in a straight line when the brain asks for nothing, and takes longer to turn or stop. Hitting an edge stops that direction. Does not apply to the random baseline.
How far a dot can see. Within this distance it finds the nearest food and the nearest water and senses which way each one lies (as an angle) and how close it is. It does not sense how much there is. Tiles that are almost empty don't count. Shorten it to make memory matter.
